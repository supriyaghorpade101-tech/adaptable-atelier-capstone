# Consultation Completeness Policy

## CC-01 Required identity of the design

Before generating a concept, confirm the garment type and the customer's intended use.

## CC-02 Required functional information

Record the accessibility need, dressing method, available assistance, mobility or dexterity considerations, and any required access points.

## CC-03 Required measurements

Routine tailoring measurements are not required to generate an initial concept. Before concept generation, ask only for a measurement when it directly affects an adaptive feature, access point, seated fit, safety, or another design decision. After concept approval, collect the measurements relevant to the selected garment and record whether they were taken standing, seated, from a fitting garment, or with assistance. A customer may mark a measurement as not applicable to their body or for dressmaker confirmation; preserve that status and never invent a value. Trousers use waist, hip, inseam, and preferred rise. Skirts use waist, hip, and preferred length. Shirts, tops, and jackets use chest, waist, shoulder width, sleeve length, and garment length. Dresses use chest, waist, hip, shoulder width, and preferred length; ask for sleeve length only when the approved design has sleeves.

For a fitted upper garment, ask whether additional chest shaping is wanted. Only when it is wanted, collect high chest, under-chest circumference, and fullest-point spacing. Never use sex or gender as a proxy for these measurements.

## CC-04 Required preferences

Confirm fit, style, colour, material or sensory preferences, fastening preferences, and desired appearance of adaptations.

## CC-05 Missing information

If design-critical information is absent, ask targeted follow-up questions before generating the concept. Do not block a concept because routine tailoring measurements are deferred. After concept approval, ask about a production measurement only when it has neither a value nor an explicit "not applicable" or "dressmaker to confirm" status. Preserve confirmed exceptions in the draft specification.

## CC-06 Conflicting requirements

If two requirements conflict, explain the conflict and its practical effect, then ask the customer to clarify priorities. Never resolve a conflict silently.

## CC-07 Evidence and citations

Every recommended adaptive feature must cite the exact `entry_id` from the Adaptive Clothing Knowledge Base. A citation is valid only when the entry supports the specific feature, intended purpose, and proposed placement for this customer. A real entry ID attached to an unsupported feature or unsuitable placement is a failed citation. Check entry limitations against the customer profile and apply pressure-aware guidance where relevant. If no entry supports the need, follow escalation rule SE-04.
