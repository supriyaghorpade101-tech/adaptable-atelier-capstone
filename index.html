Known limitations

  • The prototype handles one custom adaptive-garment consultation at a time. Run All and Evals process cases sequentially; there is no concurrent customer or designer workspace.
  • The consultation and agent outputs are English-only. The prototype has not been evaluated for translation quality, alternative languages, or communication-support needs.
  • Recommendations are limited to the 14 synthetic entries in the embedded Adaptive Clothing Knowledge Base. It is not a comprehensive clinical, textile, pattern-making, or manufacturing authority; uncovered needs are escalated.
  • The measurement guide and annotated sketch are concept visuals, not CAD drawings, production patterns, graded sizing, or validated manufacturing dimensions.
  • No customer accounts or saved profiles: The prototype does not provide customer login or save accessibility needs, measurements, or preferences between consultations. Its synthetic customer profiles are fictional records used only for repeatable evaluation testing and do not represent saved prototype users.
  • Specification delivery is simulated: The prototype can mark an approved specification as sent for human review, but it does not currently email or otherwise deliver the specification to a real customer, designer, or manufacturer.
  • The prototype runs locally on Windows with a secure launcher and OpenAI API access. Evaluation evidence is stored only in the current browser, and there is no shared database.
